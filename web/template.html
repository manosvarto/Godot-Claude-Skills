Rank ladder

Pays are for the current bet, per way, for 3, 4 or 5 matching reels in a row from the left. Ways multiply: two Sergeants on reel 1 and one on reels 2 and 3 count as 2 ways.

Bonus orders

  • 3 MedalsBonus: 10 free spins. Wilds land more often during free spins.
  • 4 MedalsMax Bonus: 12 free spins, and every Wild that lands locks in place until the bonus ends.
  • Each extraEvery Medal above 4 adds 2 more spins. 5 Medals give 14 spins.
  • Medal paysMedals also pay anywhere: 3 pay 2× bet, 4 pay 10×, 5 pay 100×.
  • WildDog Tags appear on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stand in for every rank, not for Medals.
  • Max win10,000× bet per spin or per bonus.

House math

Every cell is drawn with a cryptographic random number generator. The odds are weighted so the house keeps about 4% over the long run.
